Whales Only Exist on Weekends

Public whale sightings in Monterey Bay follow the people who log them: weekends, holidays, mid-morning, close to three harbors. Underwater microphones that recorded on most days hear whales just as often midweek. Six notebooks, one per claim, hold the SQL behind each finding and its results.

Pipeline: OBIS and iNaturalist open data, loaded into Snowflake, modelled by dbt into a star schema (one fact table, four dimensions), analysed with SQL in Snowflake notebooks and shown in Power BI.

How These Pages Were Made

Each page ran once against the star in Snowflake (WHALES.MARTS) and keeps the results it got, so nothing runs in the browser. The cells are the same as the Snowflake Workspace notebooks in the repository's analysis folder.
