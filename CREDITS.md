# Credits

This project uses only openly published data. Thank you to every observer, survey team and data publisher below.

## Whale records (OBIS)

All whale records come from the [Ocean Biodiversity Information System](https://obis.org) open-data archive (`s3://obis-open-data/`): 188,228 cetacean records from 117 datasets in the West Coast box (32 to 49.5 N, 130 to 116 W), 82,363 of them in the Monterey Bay box. Each dataset keeps its own licence: CC BY-NC 4.0 (66 datasets), CC0 1.0 (31 datasets), CC BY 4.0 (19 datasets), not stated (1 dataset). The CC BY-NC data (including Happywhale and iNaturalist) is used here for a non-commercial portfolio, with credit.

Generated by `make_credits.py` from `WHALES.MARTS` and the OBIS API records. Records are counted after dbt cleaning.
